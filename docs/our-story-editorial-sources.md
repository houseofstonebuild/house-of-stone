# House of Mabwe — Our Story editorial research and provenance

## Scope
This immersive introduction describes Zimbabwean stone sculpture in general, not a documentary of Coil's specific production. The illustrated journey between Guruve and Harare, archived carving footage of a different sculpture, and the model-derived Coil carving/finishing sequence are differentiated in the visitor-facing note. Coil's approved geometry, material and maker credit remain unchanged.

## Evidence behind “listening to the stone”
- **William Murenza**, artist statement on ZimSculpt: “Each stone is a sculpture in waiting, the stone tells me what to do.” https://zimsculpt.com/artists/william-murenza-2/ (accessed November 16, 2026). This is the attributed quotation shown in the preparation chapter; **Murenza is not represented as a House of Mabwe founding artist**.
- **Collen Nyanhongo**, first-person artist statement, West + Main, June 11, 2020: describes listening to the spirit of stone and using its physical form as creative guidance. https://www.westandmain.co/blog/meet-this-weeks-featured-artist-collen-nyanhongo
- **Gedion Nyanhongo**, interview with Celebration of Fine Art, March 31, 2026: discusses the physical shape of stone, his ideas, and the belief that the stone has its own spirit. https://celebrateart.com/meet-the-artist-gedion-nyanhongo-unearths-each-stones-unique-story/
- **Dominic Benhura**, reported interview in The National, December 31, 2019: starts with sketches before selecting a block and uses imagination and intuition once carving starts. https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts/rolling-stones-how-zimbabwean-artist-dominic-benhura-creates-his-work-1.958283
- **Terrence Musekiwa**, first-person interview with Something Curated, June 27, 2024: approaches carving as a meeting of ideas, materials and ancestral knowledge. https://somethingcurated.com/2024/06/27/terrence-musekiwa/

## Editorial rules
1. Do not describe spiritual beliefs as universal to Zimbabwean sculptors, or attribute these writers' words to House of Mabwe's represented artists.
2. Do not represent workshop footage or the animated Coil carving as footage of Coil being made.
3. Keep genuine quotes precisely attributed; research context can inform the voice without fabricating dialogue.
4. Use the original approved Coil model, and preserve individual artist credits on the collection.
5. Keep a single final call to action: **Discover the collection**, linking to `/collection`.
