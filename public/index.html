THE MAKING

Making the sculpture

Each work begins with the choice of a stone. The artist then carves, refines and finishes it by hand.

01 / SELECTION

Choosing the stone

Artists look at a block's color, grain and condition before deciding how to work with it. Stone may come from a quarry or a supplier. The source of a particular piece is recorded with its sculpture details.

02 / CARVING

Shaping the form

Hammers and chisels remove the first layers of material. Rasps and finer tools then refine the curves, openings and edges. The pace and method depend on the stone and the artist's approach.

03 / FINISHING

Finishing the surface

Sanding smooths selected areas, while other surfaces may retain their natural texture. Polishing brings out the stone's color and gives the finished work its final surface.

Sculpture for interiors

Explore stone sculpture for residential and architectural projects.

Choosing work for a space

Consider the scale of the sculpture, the materials around it and the light throughout the day. A work can sit on a console, stand on a plinth or become the focus of a room.

Share your brief, dimensions and reference images with us. We can discuss the pieces in the collection and help you assess their fit for your project.

Privacy.

Your inquiry.

When you send an inquiry, House of Mabwe receives your name, email address, interest, and message so we can respond. Inquiries are kept in a private inbox. If email notifications are enabled, they are also delivered through our email provider, Resend. Your details are used for this conversation and are not added to a marketing list.

Understanding interest.

We measure page visits, collection clicks, work views, and inquiries using a random identifier for your browser session. Activity records contain artwork identifiers and campaign information, rather than the contents of your inquiry. We do not store your IP address in activity records. Activity records are removed after one year.

Other services.

The Manrope typeface is loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser preference is stored on your device. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals turn activity measurement off.

Questions or removal.

Send an inquiry if you want to ask about your information or request that an earlier inquiry be removed.
