Privacy.

Your enquiry.

When you send an enquiry, House of Stone receives your name, email address, interest, and message so we can respond. Enquiries are kept in a private inbox. If email notifications are enabled, they are also delivered through our email provider, Resend. Your details are used for this conversation and are not added to a marketing list.

Understanding interest.

We measure page visits, collection clicks, work views, and enquiries using a random identifier for your browser session. Activity records contain artwork identifiers and campaign information, rather than the contents of your enquiry. We do not store your IP address in activity records. Activity records are removed after one year.

Other services.

The Manrope typeface is loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser preference is stored on your device. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals turn activity measurement off.

Questions or removal.

Send an enquiry if you want to ask about your information or request that an earlier enquiry be removed.
