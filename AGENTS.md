# House of Stone

Preserve the approved warm ivory gallery, charcoal typography, white hero text over the photograph, thin Manrope typography, compact centered Taper face logo, and inquiry-only acquisition flow. No prices or checkout. No public concept, generated-image, or placeholder labels.

Never reset or replace the production database during code edits or deployment. Update live content through the authenticated collection endpoints or editor.
